# Vision — school-committee

## One-Sentence Vision
A mobile-first platform helps Czech primary-school parent committees coordinate contributions, volunteer work, feedback, and transparency.

## Problem Statement
The existing documentation identifies fragmented coordination of committee finances, tasks, and feedback.

## Target Users
Parents, committee representatives, teachers, and school staff.

## Core User Need
Users need a clear way to contribute, volunteer, provide feedback, and see committee transparency.

## Key Outcomes
One school uses the service, QR contributions and manual confirmation work, a volunteer cycle completes, and a public transparency page is live.

## Non-Goals
Social feeds, child accounts, public individual payment visibility, and school-provided parent import are outside the MVP.

## Success Criteria
The MVP criteria recorded in `BUSINESS.md` are met without violating the stated legal and privacy constraints.
